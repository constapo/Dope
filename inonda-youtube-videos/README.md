## House rules for every Inonda video (owner, 2026-10-29)
- **No prices, ever.** The company does not work with fixed prices. Never show, say or caption a
  price, price range, cost figure, "from €…", "under €…" or price placeholder in any video, reel,
  short, post, thumbnail, title or description.
- **Never talk about clients' budgets** in company videos (no "on a budget", "how to build a budget",
  "what it costs", "save here / spend here", "without spending a fortune").
- What we may say instead: every job is different; we visit, look at the job properly and give a
  clear written quote (free inspection).
- Narration: Kokoro `am_michael` (speed 0.90), Greek subtitles, silent bilingual end card.
- **Real-looking footage only — never drawings, cartoons, animations or illustrated scenes**
  in any video, preview included (owner, 2026-10-29: "never-ever drawings or cartoon on videos").
- **Camera always at hand height, level, like a person filming with a phone.** No views from the
  ceiling, no top-down/overhead shots, no drone or crane angles, no low floor-level angles.
  Horizon level, slight natural hand movement (owner, 2026-10-29).
- **AI-generated footage of interior work is required and allowed** (tiling, floors, walls, kitchens,
  bathrooms), but only when it looks real and is filmed from the right angle: handheld phone at
  chest/hand height, level horizon (owner, 2026-10-29: "must be...but always from right angle of
  filming"). Generated clips from the ceiling, top-down, floor-level or drawn/cartoon are rejected.
- **Free sources only for generating footage — never paid services** (owner, 2026-10-29: "search
  and find always freewares sources on generating shoots...never paid sources"). Use free tools,
  free tiers or open models; never spend credits or money on generation.
- **Videos may be packed from many cuts** (owner, 2026-10-29). Cuts come from footage whose licence
  allows commercial reuse: free stock (Mixkit, Pexels, Pixabay, Coverr), CC0/CC-BY clips (credited),
  free-generated clips, or Inonda's own footage. Not from other people's published videos without a
  licence: reusing their clips in a company ad is usually copyright infringement and gets
  Content-ID claims on YouTube/Facebook.
- **Mixing is fine** (owner, 2026-10-29): a video can combine licensed stock cuts with generated
  shots; whatever the licensed cuts don't cover is generated for free.
